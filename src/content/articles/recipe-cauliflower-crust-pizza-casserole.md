<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<h3>Can I use frozen cauliflower rice for this casserole?</h3>
<p>Yes. Thaw it completely, then squeeze out the excess moisture using a towel. Frozen rice tends to hold more water, so squeeze thoroughly to avoid a soggy crust.</p>
<h3>What can I substitute for eggs to make this vegan?</h3>
<p>A flax egg (1 tablespoon ground flaxseed mixed with 3 tablespoons water, let sit for 5 minutes) works as a binder. The crust will be slightly more delicate, so let it cool fully before slicing.</p>
<h3>How do I prevent the crust from falling apart?</h3>
<p>The key is squeezing the cauliflower very dry—you want no visible moisture when you press the pulp. Also, let the casserole rest for 5 minutes after baking before cutting.</p>
<h3>Can I add other vegetables?</h3>
<p>Absolutely. Sautéed mushrooms, bell peppers, or spinach work well. Just cook off any excess moisture before layering to keep the crust crisp.</p>
